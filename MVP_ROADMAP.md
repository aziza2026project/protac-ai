# PROTAC Nexus MVP Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Whole-app interface (current)
- Establish brand, navigation, homepage, and service directory.
- Prepare dedicated pages for each scientific workflow.
- Prepare consultation/collaboration forms and user/admin/payment portal layouts.
- Clearly mark unavailable integrations and prevent fabricated scientific outputs.

## Phase 2 — Scientific data
- Preserve original spreadsheets as immutable raw sources.
- Profile missing values, duplicate IDs, and inconsistent units.
- Normalize compound, target, warhead, E3 ligand, linker, assay, and reference records.
- Record provenance and keep experimental evidence separate from computational predictions.

## Phase 3 — Scientific engines
- Choose and validate structure visualization and docking workflows.
- Define task-specific validation for any prediction model.
- Add linker comparison first; only add generation when chemistry constraints and evaluation are established.
- Assess ternary complexes with a suitable structural workflow and communicate uncertainty.

## Phase 4 — Production platform services
- Database, authentication, role-based permissions, secure file storage, request lifecycle, and audit logs.
- Email notifications and admin review workflows.
- Payment provider selected only after eligibility and regional requirements are confirmed.
- Server-side subscription/payment authorization, tests, deployment, backups, and privacy review.

## Scientific safeguards
- Natural origin is not evidence of efficacy by itself.
- Do not equate IC50, Ki, Kd, DC50, and Dmax.
- Docking scores are computational outputs, not proof of binding, degradation, or clinical benefit.
